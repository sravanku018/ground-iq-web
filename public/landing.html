Enterprise Architecture

How Web Surveys Integrate Seamlessly

Web surveys are designed to complement ground field enumeration without creating quota confusion or unvetted data clutter.

1
Strict Tenant Headroom Lock:

When you reserve 200 web responses, remaining field quota instantly reconciles to (5,000 − 200 − used).

2
Live Start & End Timers:

Timers activate the moment a link is minted and record the conclusion timestamp when targets are reached.

3
Instant Public Fill Experience:

Voters and citizens can respond directly on any mobile browser (Safari, Chrome) without downloading an app.

4
Unified Client Admin Review:

Web intakes land alongside field records, separated by source pills with bulk QA confirmation actions.

5
Question Categorization:

Questions are grouped into named categories that flow from the builder to the phone to the charts, keeping long surveys organized end to end.
